StickX privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. StickX is a browser extension. This policy covers the extension and the StickX server at stickx-api.fly.dev, run by Shaik Mehaboob Subahan.

In short

  • Without a StickX account, nothing leaves your browser. The companion, your page marks, notes and drawings stay on your device.
  • With an account, your StickX server keeps your email address, your settings and what you choose to save. Nothing is sent until you act (sign in, save, use AI, speak a voice command).
  • AI requests and voice commands go to Groq, using your own Groq API key, only when you ask.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

  • Your settings and the companion's state (on or off, its place, your preferences) are kept in the extension's storage in your browser.
  • Your marks on a page (ink, highlights, shapes, text labels, sticky notes, drawings) are kept in the extension's own database on your device, with the page's address (without tracking parameters), its title and its width, so they come back when you return. Websites cannot read them. You can switch this off and delete everything from the toolbar popup. Nothing is kept in incognito windows.
  • To move its eyes and nap while you are away, the companion uses the cursor position and the time of your last input on the page. These are used on the page only and never stored or sent.
  • Nothing is read from a page or sent anywhere unless you use a feature described below.

With a StickX account

An account is optional. When you have one, the StickX server keeps:

WhatDetails
Your accountEmail address, an optional display name, and a hash of your password (never the password itself).
Your sessionsA keyed hash of each session's token (never the token), times, and your browser's user-agent string, so you can tell your devices apart. No IP addresses are stored.
Your settingsSo they follow you between devices.
Your page marksOnly if you switch on "Sync page annotations" (off by default), with each page's address and title.
What you saveSelections, links, notes, AI answers and screenshots of an area you pick, with the page they came from; learning items you create (quizzes, flashcards, study notes, plans), your scores and review schedule.
Your Groq API keyIf you add it: encrypted (AES-256-GCM), and readable only by the running server to make your AI requests. The extension never stores it.

The server's logs record only the kind of request, its status and timing: never what you send, your email, tokens, keys or page addresses.

AI and voice

  • AI works only with your own Groq API key and only when you pick an action (Explain, Summarize, a question). Before anything is sent, StickX shows you what will go: your selection, the page's title and address, the page's text if you add it, or descriptions of your marks. Text in form fields is never sent.
  • Voice commands are push-to-talk only: StickX listens after you press Talk or Alt+Shift+V and stops when you stop speaking (15 seconds at most). The recording goes to Groq to be turned into text.
  • Requests travel from your browser to your StickX server and on to Groq (Groq's privacy policy). The StickX server does not store or log prompts, page text, recordings or answers.
  • Usage and billing for your key are between you and Groq.

Screenshots

StickX takes a screenshot only when you sketch or save an area of a page, and only after you started it from Chrome (toolbar button, right-click menu or keyboard shortcut) on that tab. It hides itself for that moment and keeps only the area you picked. To sketch, the picture is used and dropped; to save, the cropped image is kept in your account.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The only other service that receives data is Groq, for the AI and voice requests you make. StickX's use of information it receives follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it (toolbar popup) or remove the extension.
  • In your account you can delete any saved item, your page marks, your Groq key or your settings at any time. Deleting your account removes everything it holds at once.
  • Signing out deletes the session token from your browser and ends the session on the server.

Security

Passwords and tokens travel only over https (or to a server on your own computer). StickX's interface on a page is closed to the page's own scripts, so a website cannot read your notes or AI conversations.

Children

StickX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and changes in how StickX handles your data are announced in the extension before they apply.
