Talk to StickX

Voice commands need Chrome's permission for StickX to use your microphone. You give it once, here.

  • StickX listens only after you press Talk (in its menu) or Alt+Shift+V, and stops when you stop talking, after 15 seconds, or when you press again.
  • The recording goes to your StickX server, which turns it into text with Groq using your own key. It is not kept.
  • You can take the permission back at any time in Chrome's site settings for StickX.
